# Palette & Visual Roles

The approved palette direction is:

- **Navy** — primary structural/background color; keeps the world theatrical and readable.
- **Cream** — paper/parchment/light neutral; useful for readable cards and handmade contrast.
- **Crown Yellow** — victory, focus, host/game-show emphasis, crown symbolism and primary calls to action.
- **Coral** — warm comic accent, danger/chaos/emphasis without becoming realistic blood-red.
- **Cyan** — cool accent for magical/AI/processing energy and contrast.
- **Purple** — royal/theatrical secondary accent.

## Rules

- Do not use every palette color equally on every screen.
- Crown yellow should retain special visual importance.
- Keep readable text/background contrast higher priority than decorative palette fidelity.
- AI-processing visuals should still feel part of the castle game show rather than generic sci-fi neon UI.
- New component prototypes should document any additional colors they introduce and why.
