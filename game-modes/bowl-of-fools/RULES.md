# Bowl of Fools — Rules

## Status

These are the confirmed rules. **Bowl of Fools** is a working title. Timer, player-count and scroll-count defaults remain tuning decisions.

## Setup

- Team-based play.
- Host selects **Local** or **Remote Live**.
- Host chooses whether Round Two uses **Drawing** or **Charades**.
- Players submit the configured number of scrolls.
- All scrolls enter one shared anonymous bowl.
- Scroll authors are never revealed.

## Core turn

1. The server assigns the active team and performer.
2. The performer receives the current scroll privately.
3. Teammates call out guesses.
4. On a correct guess, the performer records **Correct** and the team gains one point.
5. The performer may use **one pass per turn**. The passed scroll returns to the bowl.
6. The turn continues until the server-owned deadline or the bowl empties.
7. The next eligible performer rotates according to the team order.

Spoken guesses are judged socially; automated speech recognition is not required.

## Rounds

The exact same scroll set returns each round.

### Round One — Describe

The performer describes the scroll using the round's displayed clue restrictions. They may not simply give the answer.

### Round Two — Drawing or Charades

The host-selected rule applies for the whole match:

- **Drawing:** communicate by drawing; no spoken clue from the performer.
- **Charades:** communicate through acting/gesture; no spoken clue from the performer.

### Round Three — One Word

The performer may give only one clue word for each scroll, then teammates guess.

## Empty bowl

When the last scroll is guessed, the round ends immediately. Reset the same scrolls for the next round. The team behind in cumulative points starts the next round.

The starting-team procedure when scores are exactly tied remains open.

## Illegal clues and correction

Clue legality uses the honor system. The host has **Undo** to reverse the affected correct ruling/point. Undo is an authoritative ledger event; it cannot silently edit only one client.

## Final result

Add points across all three rounds. If final totals are tied, play **one-scroll sudden death**. That single deciding scroll determines the winner.

## Remote Live

- Teammates guess through live voice.
- Built-in voice chat is optional and may be turned off so players can use an external phone call or other voice channel.
- Normally transmit only the current performer's camera.
- Each player controls their own view and can change it at any time.
- Media failure does not change the rules or official turn state.

## Secrecy

Never reveal scroll authors during play, results, rematches, logs or History Books.
