# Product Vision

## Product

CALLBACK / King Prompter is a mobile-first multiplayer party game built around prompts, player-created responses, voting, escalating rounds, AI-assisted content and theatrical presentation.

The game should feel easy to join, easy to understand and socially funny rather than mechanically dense.

## Audience

Groups of friends and family playing together in the same room or remotely. Players should not need gaming experience or technical knowledge.

## Primary journey

1. Host opens the game.
2. Host creates a room and selects game options.
3. Players join with a room code on their phones.
4. Players identify themselves and use/select an avatar.
5. The server moves everyone through explicit game phases.
6. Players submit, watch reveals, vote and see results.
7. Scores escalate and the crown becomes the visible symbol of competition.
8. Finale determines the winner.

## Product goals

- Produce laughter reliably across mixed-skill groups.
- Make each round understandable without long explanations.
- Keep players engaged while AI/image work occurs.
- Use callbacks, visual spectacle, avatars and the pigeon host to make the game feel like one coherent show.
- Reward creativity without making the naturally funniest person unbeatable.

## Non-goals

- Serious competitive esports balance.
- Realistic medieval simulation.
- Heavy RPG progression.
- Complex account systems as a prerequisite to playing.
- UI that feels like a generic productivity app.
